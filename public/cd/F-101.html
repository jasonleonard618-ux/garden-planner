GENERAL NOTES

  1. Fence height and apron per Source: UGA Extension C1027-9. This level keeps out: rabbits.
  2. Call 811 at least two working days before driving or digging posts. Source: call811.com
  3. Check town and HOA rules for fence height, material and setback; some require a permit over 6'-0".
  4. Fasteners and connectors in contact with pressure-treated lumber: hot-dip galvanized or stainless. Source: IRC R317.3.1
  5. Bays split each side into equal spaces no wider than shown; gate on the south-most side.
