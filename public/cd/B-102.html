GENERAL NOTES

  1. Dimensions are to outside of framing unless noted. Lumber sizes are nominal; actual sizes shown.
  2. Use cedar heartwood. Untreated lumber chosen for beds that touch food crops. (practical choice)
  3. Fasteners: exterior-rated coated or stainless screws. Where any preservative-treated lumber is used, fasteners must be hot-dip galvanized, stainless, silicon bronze or copper. Source: IRC R317.3.1
  4. Set the bed level along its length; shim with soil, not stones.
  5. Orient long side north–south where the site allows. (practical choice)

KEYNOTES

  1. 12×10 cedar side board (1 1/2" × 9 1/4" actual), 8'-0" long.
  2. 22×10 cedar end board, cut to 3'-9" (45") to fit between sides.
  3. 34×4 cedar post inside each corner, cut to bed height + 2"; bottom 2" set into soil.
  4. 4(3) #10 × 3" exterior-rated screws per board end, each face, into post. (practical choice)
  5. 56 mil poly liner on soil side of boards only; bottom left open to drain. (practical choice)
  6. 6Raised-bed soil mix; fill to 2" below top, water in to settle.
  7. 8Existing grade: strip sod, loosen native soil 6", level before setting bed.
  8. 9Drip emitter lines, 12" emitter spacing, 3 runs per bed. See drip sheet. Source: Clemson HGIC 1678
